# Fake ChatGPT & Gemini Sites Phishing Scams

![Banner](banner.jpg)

Cybercriminals are increasingly exploiting the massive popularity of generative AI by creating highly convincing fake ChatGPT and Google Gemini websites. These sophisticated phishing operations are designed to trick users into downloading infostealing malware or handing over sensitive corporate credentials under the guise of accessing premium AI features or desktop clients.\n\nOur latest security alert breaks down the anatomy of these fake AI portals, the malvertising campaigns driving traffic to them, and the crucial indicators of compromise (IoCs) organizations must monitor to protect their employees.\n\nRead the full threat analysis on our official security portal.
